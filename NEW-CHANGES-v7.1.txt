KILLA FITNESS V7.1 — TITANIUM X TRAINING EDITION

- Rebuilt the Smart Workout Generator with visual muscle-group tiles.
- One focus per session: Chest, Arms, Back, Shoulders, Legs, or Core.
- Standard, Intense, and Beast Mode selection; 30/45/60/75/90-minute sessions.
- Reworked workout preview with exercise thumbnails, equipment, rep targets and training-method badges.
- Exercise substitution and regeneration, with saved custom workout templates.
- Redesigned Program Builder with muscle-group filters and mode-based starting points.
- Existing combined programs are retained for access and backup; they are no longer the default session generator.
- Training HQ redesign; no mining imagery or work-related buttons.
- Six equal bottom navigation tabs: Home, Train, Programs, Progress, Fuel, More.
- Updated displayed version, asset cache and app title.
- Original exercise image library and local workout storage key retained.
- Technique information available from active workout method badges.

LIMITATIONS / IMPORTANT:
- Existing exercise pictures are 3D-style illustrations, not rotatable 3D models. They have not been regenerated in this update.
- Advanced methods currently offer exercise-level labels and guidance; full multi-exercise guided giant-set and superset logging is not yet implemented.
- Session duration is an estimate, not a guaranteed stopwatch limit.
- Visual styling is based on the approved mockup but not pixel-identical to a rendered concept image.
- Back up data in Settings before replacing app files.
